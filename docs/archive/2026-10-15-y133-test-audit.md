# Y-133 source-model trial statistics

The October 13 operator guarantee extends G7. The implementation projects
existing execution, plan, candidate, review, finding and approval rows into
the existing trusted attempt/project statistics. The only new durable fact
is whether and when a picker output was applied. It lives on that execution.
No new scheduler, trial entity, lease, external service or dependency is added.

Selections count distinct source publications, not calls or graph traversals.
Historical applied sources remain selected samples after supersession, with
separate current and superseded counts and explicit choice status. Repeated
operator selection of one candidate is one source sample. Publication and
eligibility counts remain distinct, including reviews with no findings.
Model groups separate attempt, ticket revision and plan revision. Full frozen
admission and input evidence permits narrower comparisons. A repair consumer
means the feedback reached a repair execution; it proves no objective truth.

review graph. Their assertions also show G7. Retain the distinct G7 usage
scenario unchanged: it already exercises absent usage, interruption, resumed
explicit zero, partial increments, cleanup and restart. All three use real

- Candidate attribution protects against reporting a picker's model as the

- Application assertions protect against counting a held ownership publication
  as applied or calling an obsolete choice current. The real inherited owner
  supplies the unapplied case. Existing plan revision and candidate replacement
  supply supersession. Repeated selection of c1 still has two source samples
  only after the replacement publication. Repair consumers and exact successful
  landing choices remain separate from selection rates.
- Cleanup/reopen comparisons protect against counting the same input or choice
  again and relying on disposable artifacts. They reuse each scenario's native
  landing and cleanup. A further daemon restart follows checked cleanup release.
