# E2E budget: one binary, and where the image time goes

Follows `2026-09-28-e2e-budget.md`. Same host (yard-sthlm, Debian 13 in a
Proxmox LXC, 4 CPUs, podman 5.4.2 on native overlay, pinfold 0.0.6), debug
build, four test threads.

## One binary

The fifteen files are now modules of one test binary,
`crates/e2e/tests/guarantees/`, so the four threads stay busy across
guarantees instead of waiting on each file's slowest scenario.

| Run | Wall |
|---|---|
| fifteen binaries, one after another (earlier note) | 24 min |
| one binary, disk at 97% | 19.6 min, 3 failed (`pinfold box list` timed out at 30 s) |
| one binary, disk at 37% | 13.0 min, 55 passed |
| one binary, after the harness removes its images | **10.0 min**, 55 passed |

## Images

Every project builds its own image, and every new target head builds it
again. pinfold gives every build a new image id (a per-build label in the
config). Measured with a `FROM pinfold/profile-default:latest` build:

| Step | Disk | Time |
|---|---|---|
| `image build`, fully cached | +0 | 3.3 s, of which 3.0 s is three retries pulling the `localhost/` base |
| first `box up` from a new id | +326 MB | 3 s idle, 14 to 19 s with four boxes starting |
| a later `box up` from the same id | +0 | 0.4 s |

Rootless podman here cannot shift ids (`Supports shifting: false`), so for
`--userns=keep-id` it makes a uid-mapped copy of the image the first time a
box starts from an id, recorded per image rather than per layer. The copy
holds the storage lock: other pinfold calls wait behind it (`box list` up
to 13 s, `box exec` up to 23 s in the baseline run).

One suite run makes 62 image builds and 143 box starts. The suite never
removed its images, and pinfold keeps a name's last images forever once the
name is no longer built: two days of runs left 377 images and 72 GB, and
the disk at 97% caused the timeouts above. The harness now removes the
image ids its projects' boxes reported, once its daemon and boxes are gone;
disk stayed flat across the 13-minute run.

fuse-overlayfs would avoid the copy (`Supports shifting: true`), but the LXC
has no `/dev/fuse`.

Filed on pinfold: #63 (per-build ids cost a mapped copy each; the
measurement #52 asked for), #64 (3 s of retries pulling a `localhost/`
base), #65 (no way to retire a caller image name).

## What the images cost the suite

The 10-minute run, every pinfold call timed from a wrapper on `PATH`:

| Call | n | Mean | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| `box up` to ready, first from its image id | 62 | 5.4 s | 335 s |
| `box up` to ready, image id seen before | 82 | 1.6 s | 129 s |
| `image build` | 62 | 4.6 s | 283 s (about 186 s the #64 retries) |
| `box exec` | 219 | 1.3 s | 277 s |

Ten minutes on four threads is about 2400 thread-seconds. First starts at
the warm cost would save about 300 of them, about 75 s of wall time, and
#64 about 45 s more. Content-addressed ids (#63) or fuse-overlayfs would
take the suite to about 8 minutes, not 5: the rest is Pi starting and
running in its boxes, gates and git. fuse-overlayfs is not pursued: that
bound does not justify the LXC change (a reboot, the snapshot freezer, and
FUSE on image-layer reads).
