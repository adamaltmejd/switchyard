# Y-107 sizing — 2026-10-12

This record is a source assessment, not implemented behavior. The worker

## Source trace

The inspected boundaries are:

- `store/attempts.rs::Attempt::plan_revision` reads the single admission
  snapshot. `attempts::insert` persists it with the attempt. There is no
  revision ledger or separately frozen ticket identity in that snapshot.
- `jobs/mod.rs::load_attempt` checks the recorded selection against its
  frozen canonical source, then `resolved` supplies selected definitions.
  A revision must remain distinguishable from original admission evidence.
- `plan.rs::Plan::ordinary_selection` compares retained nodes with ordinary
  definitions and normalizes their panel links and bounded repair routes.
  Structural validation alone does not establish runnable support.
- `jobs/mod.rs::advance` reads a plan before successor transactions.
  Gate/review starts and gate repairs guard ticket edits.
  `jobs/review.rs::blocked` guards ticket identity and round count.
  Revision CAS must also prevent these readers from acting on an old plan.
- `jobs/admit.rs::attempt_approve` loads selected policy before its decision
  transaction. `jobs/queue.rs::next` reads approval and attempt snapshots
  before admitting a landing. Both need current revision rechecks.
- `store/checks.rs::current` matches candidate, proof, ticket, whole digest
  and exact plan revision, alongside ended status and released ownership.
  Node-bound results also require checked teardown and eligibility.
- `store/executions.rs::binding` separately selects panel producers using
  exact revision equality. Retaining a check without updating this reader
  would leave new consumers unable to bind that retained producer.
- `jobs/mod.rs::selected_checks_current` rechecks the selected IDs at
  approval and queue boundaries. It must use the same qualification as
  scheduling and producer selection.
- `config.rs::gate_digest` covers all gates and the Dockerfile.
  `review_digest` covers the panel, seat agents, instructions and blocking.
  Neither alone is the requested per-node policy and typed-input identity.

These are two reviewable changes: safe revision mutation with conservative
qualification, then selective qualification shared by every consumer.
Implementing only a new operator tool and dropping revision equality would
not meet the ticket. Running/reserved effects, unresolved ownership and
landing intent must continue to refuse adjustment throughout both changes.

## Proposed sequence

Proposal 273, key `y107_quiescent_cas`, requests append-only quiescent CAS,
history, separate ticket identity and current revision checks at mutation
boundaries. It retains conservative exact-revision check qualification.
Older completed checks would rerun in that prerequisite's interim contract.
That is a proposed scope choice for the operator, not the agreed final
Y-107 behavior and not permission to weaken any existing safeguard.

Proposal 274, key `y107_selective_evidence`, depends on the first. It owns
selective qualification by candidate, node, typed producer inputs and policy,
including actual old producer bindings and approval/queue rechecks.

Proposal 275 requests both as prerequisites of Y-107. It preserves the
parent's entire body and integrated acceptance. It moves no downstream
dependency. Y-62, Y-108 and Y-109 keep their existing roles. All three
proposals await operator review; no child is admitted by this record.
